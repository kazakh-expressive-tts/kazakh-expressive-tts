Expressive Speech Samples

For each speaker we present six emotional categories — angry, fearful, happy, neutral, sad, surprised. Each row contrasts a Real sample (ground-truth recording, expressing the emotion in plain speech) with a Predicted sample synthesized by our model, where the same emotion is delivered alongside an embedded paralinguistic event (shown as [tag] within the transcript). Across the three speakers, all ten paralinguistic tags (burps, cough, gasp, groan, laugh, long pause, sigh, sniffle, whispers, yawn) are demonstrated.
